# Teaching pairs

Each pair shows one fault and its fix. Read the bad half to recognise the fault, never as a starting point.

## 1. The guessed rule (rule 2)

Bad:
> Maya: "ok"
> Written: Dark mode: near-black. (source: picked)

Good:
> Checklist: "4. Near-black, or soft dark grey?" Maya: "Soft grey, near-black is harsh."
> Written: Dark mode: soft dark grey, around #1a1a1d. Why: near-black felt too harsh. (picked). Feedback log: near-black refused, "too harsh".

Why: "ok" is not a pick; the fix even caught a wrong inference.

## 2. The mood rule (writing the skill)

Bad:
> Overall feel: clean, modern and premium.

Good:
> Edges: 1px hairlines at 8 to 12% contrast; one soft shadow only on floating surfaces. Why: "Linear's lines, Stripe's floating pricing card".

Why: an agent can't build "clean"; it builds its own default and calls it clean.

## 3. The question in CSS terms (standing rules)

Bad:
> What border-radius do you prefer for cards, in pixels?

Good:
> Corners: sharp like Linear's cards, a little soft like Vercel's, or round like Arc's?

Why: people recognise corners; few can name a pixel value that matches what they picture.

## 4. The interrogation (asking.md)

Bad:
> Why Stripe? ... And why does that matter? ... And what would that look like on a dashboard? ... And for dark mode?

Good:
> What does Stripe's gradient do for you? ("Premium without shouting.") Rule written; next question.

Why: one rung down gives the reason; four rungs make people answer on reflex.

## 5. The copied reference (rule 3)

Bad:
> Hero: centred headline, product screenshot below, logo row, like Linear's homepage.

Good:
> Hero types I'd accept: big quiet type left-aligned; split with product screenshot. Signature: tight headline tracking (from Linear).

Why: she loved Linear's type, not its layout. Copying the layout makes every project a Linear clone.

## 6. The twin projects (rule 5)

Bad:
> taste.md: brand #481DE8 everywhere, hero always split with screenshot, signature always the blur-in headline.

Good:
> taste.md: colour posture and the lists of accepted hero types and signatures. Each project's DESIGN.md picks one of each, and the brand colour for that project.

Why: taste is the constant; each project expresses it its own way.

## 7. The recommendation without a source (interview)

Bad:
> Recommended: soft shadows.

Good:
> Recommended: hairlines. Both Linear and Vercel use 1px lines instead of shadows.

Why: a recommendation without its reference reads as the person's taste when it's yours. Say "my default" when nothing supports it.
