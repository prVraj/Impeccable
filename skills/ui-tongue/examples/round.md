# Example: Round 2 (Look), Quick mode

The bar for every round message. The person, Maya, builds landing pages for developer tools and picked Linear, Vercel and Stripe in Round 1. Notes after the message explain each choice.

---

**Round 2: Look.** Five questions. Short answers are fine.

**Q5. Brand colour.** What's your brand colour, and where should it show: only on the main action, as accents in each section, or carrying whole sections?
Recommended: only on the main action. Linear and Vercel, the two sites you picked for colour, keep theirs to one button per screen.

**Q6. Headlines: which is closest?** Linear (tight, quiet sans), Stripe (big and confident), Apple (huge and centred), or an editorial serif?
Recommended: Linear. You said its headlines "don't shout but you read them first".

**Q7. Dark mode:** near-black, soft dark grey, or tinted with your brand colour?
Recommended: near-black, like Linear and Vercel.

**Q8. Edges and depth, pick one:** crisp hairline borders, soft shadows, glass blur, or flat colour blocks?
Recommended: hairlines. Both Linear and Vercel use 1px lines instead of shadows.

**Q9. Density:** lots of air and few words per screen, or rich and informative?
Recommended: my default is lots of air for landing pages; none of your sites settles it.

---

## Why it reads this way

| Part | What to learn |
| --- | --- |
| Every option is a thing she can picture | No hex, pixel or easing asked (standing rules) |
| Recommendations name the reference | Or say "my default" when none supports it (Q9) |
| Q6 quotes her own Round 1 words | The why of the recommendation is hers |
| One follow-up, only where the answer split | No follow-up on clear answers (`references/asking.md`) |
| "ok" and "next" are not answers | They return in the checklist as items 4 and 5 (rule 2) |
| Each unchosen item has its nearest alternative | A reflex yes still picks between two real things |
| Item 2 leaves the font open | A choice still open is shown, never decided silently |
